# MediaCloud Search Strategy

## Overview

News articles were retrieved from MediaCloud for two established pumped-storage
hydropower facilities:

- Alqueva, Portugal
- Le Cheylas, France

The observation period was 2018–2025.

The search strategy combined identifiers for the focal hydropower facilities and
associated installations with hydropower-related terminology. Retrieved articles
were subsequently screened for relevance to the focal hydropower system.
Duplicate, off-topic and non-substantive items were removed before analysis.

## Alqueva, Portugal

The search terms included:

- Alqueva
- Aguieira
- Alto Lindoso
- Alto Rabagão
- Frades II
- Gouvães
- Tâmega

These were combined with Portuguese hydropower-related terminology, including
terms referring to hydropower, hydroelectric facilities, dams, pumping and
storage.

**Note:** The exact MediaCloud query string used for the Alqueva search is not
available in the archived project materials. The terms above document the search
vocabulary used to construct the corpus and are not presented as an exact
reproduction of the original Boolean query.

## Le Cheylas, France

The following MediaCloud query was used:

```text
("Centrale du Cheylas" OR "Centrale Hydroélectrique du Cheylas" OR Cheylas
OR "Grand'Maison" OR "Grand Maison" OR "Barrage de Grand'Maison"
OR "Barrage de Verney" OR "Barrage du Chambon" OR "Barrage de Plan d'Amont"
OR "Barrage de Plan d'Aval" OR "Barrage de Saint-Guérin"
OR "Barrage de Tignes" OR "Barrage de Roselend" OR "Barrage de Bissorte"
OR "Barrage de Mont Cenis" OR "Barrage de La Girotte" OR "Barrage de Sautet")
AND
(hydroélectrique OR hydroelectrique OR barrage OR centrale OR usine
OR réservoir OR "ouvrage hydraulique")
```

The query combines identifiers for the focal facility and associated hydropower
infrastructure with French hydropower terminology.

## Corpus screening

Retrieved articles were screened for relevance to the focal hydropower system.
Duplicate, off-topic and non-substantive items were removed before analysis.

The final curated corpus used in the revised analysis comprises:

- Alqueva: 348 articles
- Le Cheylas: 298 articles
- Total: 646 articles

The search strategy is documented here to make the corpus construction
